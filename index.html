🎓 Placement Index
Home
🎓 What this feature does

Your Placement Index reflects your standing against defined placement criteria — including how consistently you perform and how interview-ready you are. Use this to assess your standing and evidence your case before submitting an appeal, so your application goes forward complete.

  • Tick each criterion you've met and attach proof for it.
  • Run the mock interview drill before you submit — it counts toward your score.
  • Submit to get the Placement Index score out of 100 with a breakdown per criterion.
📌 Your result is logged in the admin dashboard for the Talent Acquisition team to review.
