Here is your portfolio guidance for EduClaas learners.
M
Recommended Structure:
1. Introduction / About Me — a 3–4 sentence bio: who you are, your pillar (e.g. Digital Marketing, Data Science, Software Development), and what you're aiming for.
2. Skills — separate technical skills (tools, languages, frameworks) from soft skills.
3. Projects / Assignments — your 3–5 strongest pieces of work, each with the problem, what you did, and the outcome (quantify it where you can, e.g. "increased engagement by 20%").
4. Certifications — course completions, SkillsFuture-recognised credentials, any external certs.
5. Contact — email and LinkedIn, clearly visible, no digging required.
M
Tool 1 — Carrd
Best if: you want a clean one-page portfolio live in ~30 minutes with no design experience needed.
How: go to carrd.co → Sign Up (free tier lets you build up to 3 sites) → pick a one-page template → replace each section with your own bio/skills/projects/contact → Publish → you get a shareable carrd.co link (or connect your own domain on a paid plan).
Limits: themes can't be swapped after you start, and CSS customisation is limited — fine for a simple, fast portfolio, less fine if you want a highly custom look.
M
Tool 2 — Framer
Best if: you want a more polished, multi-page portfolio with animations and a more "designed" feel — worth the extra setup time if you're in a visual field (Digital Marketing, UX, Design-adjacent Business projects).
How: go to framer.com → Sign Up → start from a portfolio template (2,000+ available, switchable anytime) or a blank canvas → edit sections/add pages → Publish.
Limits: steeper learning curve than Carrd (expect over an hour to get comfortable), and check the current free-vs-paid plan details on their site before committing, since plans change.
M
Quick pick: need something live today with zero learning curve → Carrd. Have more time and want a portfolio that looks custom-designed → Framer. Ask me anything specific — e.g. "what should my Data Science portfolio include" or "how do I write my About Me section."
